Phase 1 · Detection

Local Detection at Junction J1

Ambulance physically arrives at Junction J1. Inductive camera/RFID flags emergency_flag = True. The 4-tier decision engine triggers Tier 1 Emergency Priority, terminating the active cross-street phase immediately and locking Westbound green for 45s.

J1 Action: Immediate Phase Pre-Emption (Tier 1)
Phase 2 · Prediction

Adjacency Event & ETA Broadcast

As the vehicle clears J1, the controller reads the sample_adjacency.csv graph topology. An emergency broadcast packet is dispatched to downstream Junction J2 (500m away) estimating arrival time:

ETA = 500m / 20 m/s = 25.0 seconds
Phase 3 · Actuation

J2 Queue Flush & Green Wave Lock

Junction J2 does not wait for the ambulance to reach it! 8 seconds before ETA, J2 pre-emptively clears cross traffic and turns green to flush residual vehicles from the lane, granting an unobstructed corridor.

J2 Result: Zero-Stop Uninterrupted Transit

Why Conventional Fixed-Time Fails

Conventional fixed-time signals operate as isolated, blind islands. When an ambulance navigates a 5-intersection corridor, it gets trapped behind red lights and standing queues at 3 to 4 intersections, accumulating 2.5 to 4.0 minutes of critical life-saving delay. Our Hadoop-informed corridor green wave coordinates adjacent intersections to pre-clear bottlenecks in advance.
